## ChatGPT Features: What Can It Actually Do?

Think of ChatGPT as a Swiss Army knife for your brain. One tool, many blades. Here are the four you will use the most.

### 1. The Chat Interface: Like Texting a Very Smart Friend

You open ChatGPT, type a message, and get an answer in seconds. No menus, no manual. That simplicity is a big reason why so many people use it.

The real power is in the conversation. You don't have to get your question perfect the first time. Just keep talking:

- *"Write a welcome email for my new client."*
- *"Make it shorter."*
- *"Now make it warmer, and add a line about our Friday call."*

ChatGPT remembers the whole chat, so each message builds on the last. Your past chats are saved on the left, ready when you need them.

> **How to use ChatGPT well (quick tip):** the more details you give, the better the answer. Say *who* you are, *what* you want, and *who it is for*. "Write a LinkedIn post" gives a boring result. "Write a LinkedIn post for freelance designers about raising prices, friendly tone, under 120 words" gives a useful one.

### 2. The Models: Different Brains for Different Plans

A "model" is the brain that writes the answers. Your plan decides which brain you get.

| Plan | Model | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | GPT-6 Luna | Light and fast. Great for everyday questions |
| **Go ($8/month)** | Light model, higher limits | Same kind of brain, with more room to work |
| **Plus, Pro, Business** | GPT-6.2 | The newest brain. Better at following detailed instructions, understanding images and researching the web |

**Heads-up:** many articles still talk about GPT-4. It is no longer the main model in ChatGPT, and GPT-4o was retired from the app in February 2026. If you see it recommended somewhere, that article is out of date.

### 3. Voice Mode: Talk Instead of Type

Tap the voice button and speak. ChatGPT answers out loud, like a phone call with a patient friend. Its newer voice system, **GPT-Live**, can listen while it talks, so you can interrupt it, just like in real life.

Try it for:

- Practicing English, French or Spanish out loud
- Rehearsing a job interview or a sales pitch
- Brainstorming ideas while you walk or cook

Free users get a lighter version of the voice model, and paid users get the full one. The older "Advanced" and "Standard" voices will be removed on December 11, 2026, and everyone moves to the new Live voice.

### 4. Vision: Show It, Don't Describe It

ChatGPT can also *see*. Upload a file or a photo, then ask about it:

- **A photo**: a menu in a foreign language, a plant, a math exercise.
- **A PDF or document**: "Summarize this contract in 5 simple points."
- **A spreadsheet**: "Find the trends in these sales numbers."

Free users can upload files and images, but with daily limits. Paid plans give you much more room.

## How Does ChatGPT Work? (No Tech Degree Needed)

Here is the one idea that explains almost everything: **ChatGPT is a prediction machine, not a database.**

A database stores facts and looks them up. ChatGPT doesn't look anything up. Picture the "next word" suggestion on your phone keyboard. You type "Good," and it suggests "morning." ChatGPT does the same thing, on a giant scale. It learned from enormous amounts of text, and it writes each answer one word at a time, choosing the word that fits best.

This is why it sounds so natural and human. It is also why it sometimes gets things wrong: it is built to write sentences that *sound right*, not to check that they *are* right.

Keep this idea in mind. It will help you understand its strengths and its limits below.

## What ChatGPT Does Best

- **Writing and editing.** Emails, blog posts, product descriptions, CVs, social media posts. It turns a messy idea into a clean first draft in seconds, and it can change the tone on demand (formal, friendly, short, persuasive).
  *Try:* "Rewrite this message so it sounds polite but firm."
- **Coding help.** Beginners can ask for a line-by-line explanation. Developers use it to write functions, find bugs and automate boring tasks. You don't need to be a programmer to ask for a simple script.
  *Try:* "Explain this code like I'm a beginner, then fix the error."
- **Learning and explaining.** It breaks hard topics into simple steps, gives examples, and never gets tired of "but why?" You can even ask it to quiz you.
  *Try:* "Explain inflation with a pizza example, then ask me 3 questions."

## Where It Struggles

- **Hallucinations (confident mistakes).** Since it predicts words, ChatGPT can invent facts, numbers, quotes or sources, and say them with full confidence. Newer models make fewer errors, but none are perfect. *Always double-check anything important: figures, legal points, medical advice.*
- **Knowledge limits.** ChatGPT learned from data up to a certain date, so it can miss recent news. It can search the web to catch up, but it doesn't always do it on its own. For anything recent, ask it directly to search.
- **Privacy concerns.** On Free, Plus and Pro, OpenAI may use your chats to train its models by default. You can switch this off in *Settings → Data Controls → "Improve the model for everyone."* **Temporary Chat** is another option: it doesn't appear in your history, though OpenAI keeps it for up to 30 days for safety. Golden rule: never type passwords, bank details or private client data.
## ChatGPT Pricing: How Much Does It Cost in 2026?

ChatGPT has more plans than ever. The good news: you can start for free and only pay when you actually need more. Prices below are in US dollars and come from OpenAI's pricing page and help center (late November 2026). They can change, so always check the price shown at checkout.

### Free ($0): Perfect for Trying It Out

Free is a real plan, not a short trial. You get:

- Unlimited everyday text chats with the lighter GPT-6 Luna model
- Web search, image uploads and file uploads
- Basic image creation and voice chat

The catch is the limits. Uploads, image creation, voice, deep research, memory and coding tools all have daily caps, and ChatGPT tells you when you hit one. OpenAI also says free accounts may show ads.

### Go ($8/month): A Small Step Up

Go is the budget plan. It keeps the same everyday model as Free, but gives you more room:

- More messages with tools
- More uploads and image creation
- More voice chats
- Longer memory

Go is for people who like ChatGPT but keep bumping into the free limits. It does **not** include the strongest model (GPT-6.2), and OpenAI's pricing page says it may include ads. Go is billed monthly, and its price can vary by country.

### Plus ($20/month): The Sweet Spot for Most People

Plus is the plan most regular users pick. It includes everything in Go, and adds:

- **GPT-6.2**, the newest and strongest chat model
- Advanced reasoning for harder problems
- Expanded deep research, uploads and memory
- Projects, scheduled tasks and access to custom GPTs
- More use of Codex (the coding agent) and early access to new features

If you use ChatGPT for your work every day, this is usually the plan that makes sense.

### Pro ($100 to $500/month): For Heavy Users Only

Pro is now a family of three tiers. OpenAI's pricing page lets you choose your usage level, and the monthly price runs from $100 to $500. All of them include **GPT-6 Pro-level reasoning**, much bigger limits, and the longest coding and work sessions. The higher you go, the more you can run before hitting a cap.

Who needs Pro? Mostly:

- Developers who run coding agents all day
- Researchers who use deep research constantly
- Professionals who hit Plus limits every single week

If you are not in one of these groups, you almost certainly don't need Pro.

### Business ($25 per user/month)

Teams can use ChatGPT Business, which adds shared workspaces and admin controls. By default, OpenAI does not use Business data to train its models.

## Is ChatGPT Worth Paying For?

**Short answer: it depends on how often you use it.**

- **Beginners and curious users:** stay on **Free**. It's more than enough to learn, ask questions and write simple texts.
- **Daily users, freelancers and professionals:** get **Plus ($20)**. You get the best model, higher limits and the full toolset. If ChatGPT saves you even one hour a month, it pays for itself.
- **Free is almost enough, but not quite:** try **Go ($8)** first.
- **Pro:** only if you keep hitting Plus limits. Don't start here.

**Money tip:** upgrade for one month, test it on real work, and cancel if you don't use it enough. Plans are monthly, so you are never locked in.
